## VI. Conclusion

### 6.1 Key Findings

1. **Global attitudes show clear polarization**: Significant divisions within Western countries and within Arab world
2. **Support for Iran increasing**: Despite major military strikes, international sympathy for Iran has risen, especially among Arab public and European citizens
3. **Anti-war sentiment strengthening**: As war continues and economic impacts emerge, anti-war sentiment increases in both Europe and U.S.
