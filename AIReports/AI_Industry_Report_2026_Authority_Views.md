## I. Core Perspectives from Industry Leaders

### 1. Sam Altman (OpenAI CEO)

**On AGI Timeline**

- "AGI is pretty close, but the world isn't prepared"
- "We're approaching an AGI era closer than expected"

**On OpenAI Development**

- Announced $110 billion funding round in February 2026 (historical record)
- $730 billion valuation
- Strategic partnerships with Amazon, NVIDIA, SoftBank
- Secured Pentagon contract for classified networks

### 2. Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO)

**On AGI Timeline**

- Predicts AGI within 1-3 years: "a nation of geniuses in a data center"
- "We are near the end of the exponential"

**On AI Safety & Employment**

- AI will eliminate half of entry-level white-collar jobs
- AI safety stance led to Trump administration ban on Anthropic
- Criticized OpenAI and Google's "AI race panic" strategy
- Warning: If AI growth forecasts are off by just one year, "then you go bankrupt"

### 3. Demis Hassabis (Google DeepMind CEO)

**On AGI Timeline**

- Predicts AGI by 2026-2027

**On Google AI Strategy**

- Four-step plan to return Google to "golden era"
- Gemini 3 launch in November 2025 significantly narrowed gap with OpenAI

### 4. Jensen Huang (NVIDIA CEO)

**On AI Infrastructure**

- "AI is going to fundamentally change how we compute everything"
- "The markets got it wrong—AI agents will actually increase use of software tools"
- "$660 billion AI infrastructure investment is sustainable"
- "As long as AI companies continue to generate profits, they will keep doubling"
- "AI factories are redefining the entire technology stack for the first time in 60 years"

**On Space AI**

- "In 36 months, the cheapest place to put AI will be space"

### 5. Satya Nadella (Microsoft CEO)

**On AI Development Stage**

- "2026 will be pivotal to AI's transition from discovery to diffusion"
- "Focus is moving from model demos to reliable, real-world systems"
- "AI as cognitive amplifier will transform 2026"

**On AI Inclusion**

- "We need to act with urgency to address the growing AI divide"
- Committed $50 billion to tackle AI inequality

### 6. Mark Zuckerberg (Meta CEO)

**On AI Coding**

- "AI will write most Meta code in 18 months"
- "2026 will be a major year for AI"
- "The world is going to get a lot funnier, weirder, and quirkier"

### 7. Bill Gates (Microsoft Founder)

**On AI Development Ceiling**

- "There is no upper limit on how intelligent AIs will get"
- "AI will surpass human intelligence without hitting a plateau"

**On AI Bubble & Employment**

- "Most AI companies will fail"
- AI will change society and jobs faster than expected
- Programming jobs "will be bumpy" but "enjoy the ride"

**On AI Risks**

- AI could be weaponized
- Need to address bioterrorism risks from AI

### 8. Jeff Bezos (Amazon Founder)

**On AI Investment**

- "I'm putting a lot of time in at Amazon to help it become an AI winner"
- "AI is currently in an 'industrial bubble' but the technology is real and will bring big benefits"

**On AI Impact**

- "Many jobs we've thrown human beings at for the last 20-30 years won't need as many humans"
- Launched Project Prometheus, $6.2 billion investment in AI manufacturing

### 9. Elon Musk (xAI/Tesla/SpaceX CEO)

**On AI & Space**

- "In 36 months, the cheapest place to put AI will be space"
- "Those who live in software land are about to have a hard lesson in hardware"

**On AGI**

- xAI's Grok model developing rapidly

### 10. Tim Cook (Apple CEO)

**On Apple AI Strategy**

- "One of the most profound opportunities of our entire lifetime"
- "New categories of products and services enabled through AI"
- "Visual Intelligence" as core for wearable AI devices

## III. Summary Analysis of Authority Views

### Consensus Views

1. **AGI Timeline**: Most leaders believe AGI possible within 1-3 years
2. **AI Agents Rise**: 2026 is the "Year of AI Agents"
3. **Infrastructure Investment**: Continued significant growth, no sign of slowing in short term
4. **Employment Impact**: Entry-level white-collar jobs will be significantly impacted

### Divergent Views

1. **AI Bubble**: Bill Gates warns most AI companies will fail; Jensen Huang believes investment is sustainable
2. **AI Safety**: Anthropic prioritizes safety; OpenAI prioritizes speed
3. **Regulatory Attitude**: Tech giants seek cooperation; some companies face government restrictions

### Emerging Trends

1. **Space AI**: Elon Musk proposes space AI cost advantages in 36 months
2. **AI Inclusion**: Microsoft $50 billion investment to address AI divide
3. **Visual Intelligence**: Apple and Meta focusing on AI hardware and visual recognition
