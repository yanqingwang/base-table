# Chinese AI Landscape Report: June 1 - July 5, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

The period from June 1 to July 5, 2026 saw sustained momentum across the Chinese AI ecosystem: major model releases from Zhipu AI (GLM-5.2) and ByteDance (Seed AI scaling-law research), landmark IPOs and funding rounds (Unitree Robotics, Kuaishou Kling AI at $3B, Momenta), a significant new regulatory framework for humanlike AI interaction services taking effect July 15, and escalating China-US technology tensions around AI chip controls and spyware accusations. Chinese humanoid robotics and AI video generation emerged as particularly hot sectors.

---

## 1. Major AI Model Developments

### 1.1 Baidu (ERNIE)
- **Status:** No major ERNIE 5.x model releases reported in this period. Baidu participated as an investor in Kuaishou's Kling AI $3B funding round alongside Alibaba Cloud and Tencent.
- Source: SCMP, July 3, 2026

### 1.2 ByteDance (Doubao / Seed AI)
- **New Scaling Law Discovery:** ByteDance's Seed AI team published a research paper on July 2 revealing a new scaling law for AI agents: agents can **double their learning speed every three months** by interacting with real-world environments over extended periods. The team developed **EdgeBench**, a benchmarking suite with 134 ultra-long-horizon tasks each requiring 12+ hours of continuous operation.
- **Agent Feature Disabled:** Doubao informed users on July 4 that its agent feature would go offline on July 15 due to new Beijing regulations on humanlike AI interaction services. After October 15, related data will be handled per privacy policy.
- **Seedance 2.0:** ByteDance's AI video model (released February 2026) continues to disrupt Hollywood, with lifelike video generation raising concerns about job displacement in film/TV production.
- Sources: SCMP, July 4, 2026; SCMP, July 5, 2026

### 1.3 Alibaba (Qwen / Tongyi / Damo Academy)
- **Qwen Agent Feature Disabled:** Alibaba's Qwen issued notice on July 4 that "humanlike interactive agents and user-created agent functions" will be disabled on July 10, with broader Qwen agent functions going offline July 15 — matching the effective date of new Beijing regulations.
- **Damo Academy - AI for Science:** Alibaba's Damo Academy unveiled "Elements Claw," an AI agent capable of discovering new superconductors, demonstrating AI-driven scientific materials research.
- **Claude Code Ban:** Alibaba banned employees from using Anthropic's Claude Code after discovering hidden code that could secretly track Chinese users. Listed as "high-risk software" with vulnerabilities.
- **Kling AI Investment:** Alibaba Cloud participated in Kuaishou's $3B Kling AI funding round.
- Sources: SCMP, July 3-5, 2026

### 1.4 Tencent (Hunyuan)
- **Status:** No major Hunyuan model announcements in this period, but Tencent participated as an investor in Kuaishou's Kling AI $3B funding round.
- Source: SCMP, July 3, 2026

### 1.5 Zhipu AI (GLM-5 / Z.ai / Knowledge Atlas Technology)
- **GLM-5.2 Released (June):** Zhipu launched GLM-5.2, hailed as another "DeepSeek moment" for Chinese open-source AI. The model was made open-source and received significant attention from Silicon Valley.
- **ZCode Harness (July 2):** Zhipu introduced ZCode, a harness/tool for GLM-5.2 that turns it into an autonomous coding assistant/agent, directly competing with Anthropic's Claude Code.
- **Promotions:** 50% increased data quotas for existing subscribers + 5 million free tokens for new ZCode users.
- **Positioning:** Zhipu is positioning as a champion for open-weight models amid developer frustrations with Anthropic's restrictions.
- Source: SCMP, July 2, 2026

## 3. Chinese AI Regulation and Policy

### 3.1 Interim Measures for AI Anthropomorphic Interaction Services
- **Effective Date:** July 15, 2026
- **Scope:** Covers AI services that "simulate human personality traits, thinking patterns and communication styles to provide sustained emotional interaction"
- **Key Requirements:** Services must avoid risks including extremist ideas, privacy leaks, harm to physical/mental health, and dependence/addiction
- **Exclusions:** Customer service bots, knowledge Q&A, workplace assistants, education and scientific research tools (as long as they don't involve sustained emotional interaction)
- **Impact:** ByteDance (Doubao) and Alibaba (Qwen) are disabling custom agent features in response
- Source: SCMP, July 5, 2026

### 3.2 Other Policy Developments
- **US-China Military AI:** UN think tank calls for pragmatic dialogue on military AI as global rules stall (SCMP, July 3)
- **US Lawmakers Push Tax Changes:** Rep. Nathaniel Moran leads push to deny tax incentives for US companies reliant on Chinese technology (SCMP, July 4)
- **Shanghai Quantum Computing Hub:** Shanghai unveiled a quantum computing hub, with up to 100 million yuan ($13.8M) to fund R&D (SCMP, July 2)

## 4. China-US AI Chip Restrictions Impact

- **AI Computing Stock Panic:** Fears of AI hardware glut over Meta rumor called "erroneous" by SemiAnalysis, noting Meta's compute procurement will accelerate, not slow down (SCMP, July 3)
- **Anthropic Spyware Controversy:** Anthropic embedded hidden code in Claude Code to track Chinese users — discovered by security researchers, leading to widespread backlash. Alibaba banned Claude Code internally (SCMP, July 3)
- **US Lawmakers:** Push to reduce corporate tax breaks for reliance on Chinese technology, targeting the "toxic relationship" with Beijing (SCMP, July 4)
- **Overall:** Chip restriction impacts continue to shape the landscape, with Chinese firms accelerating domestic alternatives and open-source development

## 5. Chinese AI Unicorns, IPOs & Funding Rounds

### 5.1 Unitree Robotics IPO
- **IPO Amount:** 4.202 billion yuan ($619.8 million)
- **Exchange:** Shanghai STAR Market
- **Milestone:** First publicly listed humanoid robotics company (China's first)
- **Review Speed:** Fastest under STAR Market pre-review — application accepted March 20, approved July 2 (104 days, 73 days for hearing)
- **Use of Funds:** Expand production of humanoid robots, strengthen embodied AI R&D, build new manufacturing facilities
- **Global Reach:** G1 humanoid robots deployed at Tokyo's Haneda Airport for Japan Airlines ground handling trials through 2028
- **Industry Context:** China had 140+ humanoid robot manufacturers in 2025, shipments of 14,400 units (84.7% of global market), 330+ product models by 2025
- **Market Impact:** Shares of Chinese robotics companies surged; more than 40 stocks hit daily limits; Robot ETF net capital inflows for second consecutive day
- Source: China Daily / SCMP, July 4, 2026

### 5.2 Kuaishou Kling AI Funding
- **Amount:** ~$3 billion (largest in the world by an AI video model firm)
- **Structure:** First external fundraising round, filed to HKEX
- **Investors:** Baidu, Alibaba Cloud, Tencent, Beijing Information Industry Development Investment Fund, Beijing Artificial Intelligence Industry Investment Fund, Huace Film & TV, Mango Investment
- **Valuation:** Previously reported at $18 billion valuation
- Source: SCMP, July 3, 2026

### 5.3 Momenta IPO
- **Amount:** $751 million offering
- **Exchange:** Hong Kong
- **Subscription:** Oversubscribed 414 times (nearly 210,000 orders)
- **Significance:** Year's second "red-chip" firm with Beijing's permission to list via offshore structure
- **Focus:** Autonomous driving technology
- Source: SCMP, July 3, 2026

### 5.4 Other Notable Events
- **Shanghai Quantum Computing Hub:** Up to 100 million yuan ($13.8M) in R&D funding (SCMP, July 2)
- **Hetao Innovation Hub (Hong Kong):** 2 wet lab buildings fully leased; ~90 firms signed up, mainland Chinese companies >50% of tenants (SCMP, July 4)
- **Hong Kong Assets Hit Record:** US$5.38 trillion on renewed China appetite; net fund inflows up nearly 200%, supported by tech-led stock rally (SCMP, July 2)

## 6. AI Application Landscape & Consumer Adoption

### 6.1 AI Skills in the Job Market
- **AI skills required for 4 out of 10 graduate jobs in China**, per recruitment portal data (SCMP, July 3)
- Hong Kong graduates at crossroads as AI takes over entry-level jobs — net employment outlook for Q3 2026 plunges to -9% (20pp lower than Q2) per ManpowerGroup (SCMP, July 4)
- Employee training hours in Hong Kong hit 14-year high (19.4 hours average in 2025); mastering generative AI surged from 6th priority to 2nd (49% of employers) (SCMP, July 3)

### 6.2 AI Video Generation
- **Kuaishou Kling AI** raising $3B to compete in global AI video sector
- **ByteDance Seedance 2.0** sending shock waves through Hollywood
- Competition intensifying between Chinese and US AI video models

### 6.3 Robotics & Embodied AI
- **Unitree IPO** catalyzing the sector; more than half of China's provinces have incorporated embodied intelligence into government work reports in 2026
- China has established 27 data-collection sites nationwide to support training of embodied AI systems
- Chinese humanoid robots making significant strides in motor control capabilities and "brain" evolution (hierarchical models and end-to-end models)

### 6.4 AI in Science
- **Alibaba Damo Academy** AI agent (Elements Claw) discovers new superconductors
- AI transforming materials science discovery

### 6.5 AI-Defined Vehicles
- Chinese EV makers among first to embrace AI-defined vehicles, transforming in-car experience for drivers and passengers (SCMP Plus, July 2)

### 6.6 Consumer AI Agent Landscape
- ByteDance Doubao and Alibaba Qwen disabling custom agent features due to July 15 regulations
- Move affects user-created agents for specific tasks, skills, speaking styles, role-playing characters, companions
- Zhipu AI aggressively recruiting developers with free tokens and increased quotas
